# 📘 Ransomware Playbook Summary

This summary highlights key phases and responsibilities defined in the Ransomware Incident Response Playbook.

## 🔁 Workflow Phases

1. **Investigation**: Identify scope, variant, and impact.
2. **Containment**: Stop the spread (network isolation, IOC deployment).
3. **Eradication**: Remove ransomware, patch systems, restore clean data.
4. **Communication**: Notify teams/stakeholders legally and clearly.
5. **User Action**: Educate users and guide reporting behavior.
6. **Help Desk Response**: Guide support staff for ticketing, recovery, feedback.
